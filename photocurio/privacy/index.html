PhotoCurio Privacy Policy

PhotoCurio for iPad and Apple TV, from Salt Air Works.

Last updated: September 15, 2026

PhotoCurio, from Salt Air Works, is a photo-frame app designed to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

Photos

PhotoCurio displays albums from your Photos library on your device only. Photos are never uploaded, copied, or shared by the app. If you mark a photo as a favorite from the frame, that change is made in your own Photos library and syncs through iCloud like any other favorite. Photos you hide from the slideshow are remembered by the app on your device (a list of which photos, not the photos themselves); nothing changes in your Photos library.

Location

If you enable weather overlays, PhotoCurio requests your approximate (coarse) location and sends it to Apple's WeatherKit service to retrieve local weather and forecast. Location is requested only when needed, is never stored by the app or used for any other purpose, is not linked to your identity, and is never used for tracking.

Camera (Presence Detection)

If you turn on the optional Presence Detection feature, PhotoCurio uses the front camera to notice when someone is in the room, so it can wake the frame and let it sleep when the room is empty. Camera images are analyzed on your device and immediately discarded. Nothing from the camera is recorded, saved, uploaded, or shared.

Local network

If you enable Remote Control, PhotoCurio serves a small control page to other devices on your local Wi-Fi network so they can control the slideshow. This stays on your local network; no data is sent to us or to the internet.

The page's address contains a private link that is generated on your device and shown only in the QR code and address in Settings. Devices on your network that do not have the link cannot see the slideshow's status (such as the date and place of the photo on screen) or control it. Tapping Reset Remote Link in Settings creates a new link and disconnects any device using the old one. The link is stored only on your device.

Help

When you open Help in Settings, PhotoCurio downloads the latest answers from support.saltairworks.com, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with visiting any website, that server receives your device's IP address and a standard request for the page. PhotoCurio sends nothing about you, your photos, or your settings, and nothing is downloaded until you open Help. GitHub, which runs the servers, may keep a record of the request under its own privacy statement. If the download doesn't work, Help shows the answers built into the app.

No tracking or analytics

PhotoCurio contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software, and no third-party SDKs. There are no user accounts, and we do not collect personal information. (Crash reports you choose to share with developers reach us through Apple, aggregated and not linked to your identity.)

Children

PhotoCurio is safe for all ages and collects no personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
